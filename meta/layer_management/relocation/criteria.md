# Relocation Criteria

## Core Criteria

- The abstraction level is close, but the responsibility is wrong.
- The material is a specific condition rather than a general structure.
- The responsibility has drifted, such as a rule being written as an
  explanation, or an explanation being written as a condition.

## Typical Relocations

### `core -> context`

- It depends on family, work, life history, or interpersonal history.
- It contains conditional risks or personal profile information.
- It cannot function as a general structure when handed to someone else.

### `playbook -> context`

- It depends strongly on a specific household, job, or relationship.
- It is difficult to transfer to another operator.
- It reads more like a fixed condition than an operational rule.

### `core -> theory`

- It is explanation or conceptual development rather than a general definition.
- It is useful as outward-facing writing, but not part of the minimal kernel.
- Removing it does not break the minimal OS definition.
